Swallow Falls main entrance is closed. Verified from Maryland DNR on May 18, 2026 — entrance road shut since March 9 for bridge replacement. Paving project runs May 1 → Dec 2.
Walk-in option · Fisherman's Trail. The official DNR alternate. Park at the small Fisherman's Trail lot east of the closed bridge → walk in on the Youghiogheny's east bank → see 3 of the 4 waterfalls (Muddy Creek, Lower Swallow, Upper Swallow). 4-mile round trip. Not ideal for 6 kids — Cherry Creek Loop remains the right family call. But if 2–3 adults want an early morning Sunday side mission, it works.

Fishing · light add-on

Bring a rod, fish 30 min after the Sunday hike. No full boat day needed.

  • License (adults 16+): Non-resident 3-day non-tidal ~$35 · covers Fri–Mon. Buy online at compass.dnr.maryland.gov, save PDF to phone. Kids under 16 free.
  • What bites in late May: smallmouth + largemouth bass, bluegill / sunfish (easy bites for kids), yellow perch, chain pickerel.
  • Best shore spot: Cherry Creek Cove — on the Sunday hike loop. Pack a rod in the car.
  • Backup spots: Deep Creek Lake State Park beach, Rt. 219 bridge access on Lake Shore Drive.
  • Gear rental · reality check: there's no rod/reel rental shop in McHenry. Bill's Marine rents fishing boats (not gear), High Mountain Sports rents kayaks (not gear), Johnny's Bait House sells but doesn't rent.
  • Cheapest play if you don't have rods: grab a Zebco kids combo at Walmart McHenry for $15–25 (pre-spooled, hooks + bobber included). Disposable, or keep for the next trip. One combo per 2 kids is enough — they take turns.
  • Live bait + tackle: Johnny's Bait House · Garrett Hwy · 5 min from cabin · open 6 AM weekends for the early-morning crew.
  • Skip the boat: Bill's Marine fishing boats run $300–400/day. Not worth it for this trip — Cherry Creek Cove from shore is enough.
  • Skip the trout stamp — extra $30, only needed for trout. Not necessary here.
