5 cars · how this works

Each family drives their own. No caravan.

  • Departures: target 9 AM Saturday but each family leaves when ready. Group text on departure + arrival.
  • Lunch: Sheetz Cumberland on your own clock. Don't try to sync.
  • Grocery run: someone will run to Walmart McHenry as needed — no pre-assignment.
  • Cabin parking: confirmed — driveway fits all 5 cars.
  • Sunday flexibility: 5 cars means families can split — some hike Cherry Creek, others go straight to Wisp Mountain Park. Reconvene for dinner.
  • Monday departures: stagger by 30 min to avoid 5-car convoy on I-68. Or sync if you want to caravan.
Swallow Falls is closed. Maryland's iconic waterfall hike has been shut for bridge construction since March 9, 2026. Paving project runs May 1 → Dec 2. If anyone suggests it, redirect to Cherry Creek Loop. Day-use access may have partially reopened — only worth checking by calling Herrington Manor at 301-334-9180 the Friday before.
Optional Monday stop. Antietam National Battlefield (Sharpsburg MD, off I-70) is on the way home and free. Quiet, walkable, Memorial Day–appropriate. 30 min detour, adds ~90 min total. Only if kids still have energy.

Pack from home

Stuff Walmart McHenry will not carry

  • Indian pantry: curry leaves, ginger garlic paste, asafoetida, sambar powder, rasam powder, mustard seeds, dal, atta, chana, rice if you're picky.
  • Pre-marinated proteins / paneer / readymade rotis in a cooler — saves an hour of prep Saturday night.
  • Spice tin + pressure cooker if the cabin kitchen lacks one. (Most do not.)
  • Kid gear: swimsuits, water shoes, light jackets (mountain mornings), small backpacks for the hike.
  • Bug spray — black flies and ticks active in May at elevation.
  • Cards, board games, soccer ball for fire pit / lawn time.
  • S'mores kit: Hershey's, marshmallows, graham crackers, long roasting sticks (3 minimum).
  • Drinks for the weekend: bottled water, juice for kids, beer / wine / spirits for adults — buy in VA before leaving (cheaper, easier than a Walmart liquor run on Memorial Day Saturday).
  • Reusable water bottles per person.
